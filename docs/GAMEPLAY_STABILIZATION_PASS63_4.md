# Pass 63.4 — Player-feedback gameplay and narrative follow-up

## Why
A complete live playtest with Kokoro narration succeeded, but the user reported:
static dice faces during animation, QTE scene-continuity leaks, word reveal jumping
when speech begins/ends, recurrent small-scale repetition, punishing critical streaks,
War feeling house-biased, generic lethal endings, short re-sync timers and sparse
Arcade SFX. The priority is the gameplay loop, not a dashboard redesign.

## Implemented
- Dice presentation cycles 1–20 during the existing CSS roll animation and
  **always settles on the original server-committed roll**. Intermediate faces
  are visual only; no extra dice checks or rerolls occur.
- War deals a fresh randomized 52-card shuffled deck rather than deriving a
  repeatable shuffle from the adventure turn number. Pot ordering is randomized
  on each battle. War still triggers **only** when the two face-up ranks match.
  A terminal stalemate with two equal ranks and no cards left now uses an
  unbiased tie-break coin flip instead of always picking the player. Winning
  odds are never modified based on account, difficulty or story outcome.
- Resync alert waits 90 seconds of writing rather than 45. If Story Ready has
  reached zero but failed to exit, it offers recovery after 10 seconds.
  Retryability and the 3-second normal countdown are unchanged.
- Story text never switches to a fully exposed sentence display as soon as TTS
  begins or replays the word animation when TTS ends. It follows the same
  cumulative word-reveal state and uses the existing approximate sentence cue
  to catch up with the spoken position. Narration generation, lookahead, and
  S3 caches are unchanged; audio and text remain independent when speech fails.
- Arcade feedback cues use the existing opt-in Audio Director and Arcade volume
  mixer. No audio files, GPU work or browser permissions were added.
- Story Director instruction emphasizes scene-scale developments, time jumps,
  plot progress on good rolls, meaningful new setbacks on failed rolls, and
  legitimate breathers during prolonged crises. This guides a generative model
  rather than pretending prose quality can be guaranteed by a local test.
- QTEs are temporary one-turn *mechanical* interruptions only: no mandatory
  final-paragraph cliffhanger, canonical scene alteration or QTE recollection
  in subsequent Director context. Also remove the old client-side QTE
  consequence panel beside the next choice menu. The established one-turn +/-
  buffs persist.
- Server-authoritative fatal-turn reconciliation replaces contradictory
  successful endings with a distinct epitaph based on the real recorded death
  cause. It never changes HP, resurrects Heroes or invents successful checks.

## What is deliberately unchanged
- D20 is generated by server-side `secrets.randbelow(20) + 1`. Consecutive
  natural 1 results are possible; **do not skew or secretly reroll** them.
  A pair of specified independent 1s is 1/400, but the odds of seeing any
  adjacent critical pair grow over many rolls and across party members.
- Existing Hero injury, level, death and graveyard rules remain authoritative.
- Kokoro/G6f, preview MP3s, Story Time leases, backend stacks, models and CDK
  deployment scripts remain unchanged.
- Hero resting and healing remain a separate progression feature after this
  gameplay stabilization cycle.

## Test checklist after deploying
1. New short solo adventure: resolve 4–6 choices, including success and failure.
   Verify choices change scene/objective rather than repeating solved tasks.
2. Observe D20 shuffle numbers followed by the exact final server receipt.
3. See story words reveal once, during/without narration and after a refresh.
   Narration must not wait for the visual effect to finish.
4. Trigger a QTE, then confirm next scene never appends QTE result as a last
   paragraph. The temporary effect may still appear in Hero status.
5. Play several War rounds. Equal opening ranks alone cause War; the first
   randomized deck should be different on a newly started game. A short loss
   streak is possible—report long-run outcomes if bias persists.
6. Verify Arcade audio after choosing **SFX ON**, independently adjustable for
   Arcade volume. Narration audio remains independent.
7. Wait 50 seconds during normal Director writing. No unnecessary Re-sync
   warning should appear, but it remains accessible on a genuine long stall.
8. Test a fatal finale. It must use the real recorded cause with a clear,
   distinctive final epitaph and must not show a contradictory survival scene.
9. Repeat a complete co-op run (including reconnect), noting performance and
   errors. Browser tests cannot substitute for live multiplayer acceptance.

## Operations
Install with the standard `scripts/install-overlay.sh` tool, then run the
Mac `scripts/release-staging.sh` release gate. This pass is frontend/Python
application source only; no CDK infrastructure or narrator cutover required.
