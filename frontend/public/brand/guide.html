05 / The boundaries

Restraint is the signature

DO

Preserve original artworks. Use real HTML for body/UI text. Give logo safe space, choose the supplied variant with sufficient contrast, retain the six e-ink themes.

DON'T

Recolor the gold AI, distort the Q, fabricate gradients, crowd the header with splatters, apply rectangle feathering, or use a seal on every button.
